Patent Filed Main Project

AI Smart Energy Guardian

AI-Based Energy Monitoring & Low-Cost Power Quality Analysis System

AI Smart Energy Guardian is an innovative energy monitoring platform that combines Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) with a low-cost single-phase and three-phase Power Quality Analyzer. The system is designed to provide intelligent insights into energy consumption and power quality through a unified hardware and software platform.


The NILM component enables appliance-level energy monitoring by analyzing overall electrical consumption, helping identify individual appliance usage without requiring dedicated sensors for each appliance. This provides a convenient and scalable approach to understanding energy consumption patterns.


The integrated Power Quality Analyzer, developed using Raspberry Pi 5 and the ADE9000 Energy Metering IC, enables real-time monitoring of key electrical parameters including voltage, current, frequency, power factor, energy, and harmonics. The analyzer is designed as a cost-effective alternative to conventional commercial Power Quality Analyzers, which can cost approximately ₹5–8 lakh.


The project brings together AI-based energy analysis, power-quality monitoring, embedded systems, and modern software interfaces to create an affordable and practical solution for intelligent energy management.

Key Features
  • NILM-based appliance-level energy monitoring
  • Single-phase and three-phase Power Quality Analysis
  • Real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, power factor, energy, and harmonics
  • Raspberry Pi 5 and ADE9000 Energy Metering IC based architecture
  • SPI-based data acquisition between hardware modules
  • Real-time web dashboard designed and developed entirely by me
  • Mobile application interface designed and developed entirely by me
  • Cost-effective alternative to high-priced commercial power-quality analyzers
  • Integrated platform combining energy intelligence and power-quality analysis

AI Smart Energy Guardian — Low-Cost Power Quality Analyzer

AI Smart Energy Guardian is an innovative, cost-effective Power Quality Analyzer built around the Raspberry Pi 5 and ADE9000 Energy Metering IC. The system supports both single-phase and three-phase electrical measurement, enabling real-time monitoring of key parameters including voltage, current, frequency, power factor, energy consumption, and harmonics.

The platform also incorporates Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM) for appliance-level energy analysis, providing intelligent insights into energy consumption without requiring dedicated monitoring hardware for every appliance. The system combines power-quality analysis, energy monitoring, embedded technology, and IoT capabilities within a unified, low-cost platform.

Designed as an affordable alternative to conventional commercial Power Quality Analyzers, the system aims to make advanced electrical monitoring significantly more accessible, with potential cost savings of approximately ₹5–8 lakh compared with high-end commercial alternatives.
