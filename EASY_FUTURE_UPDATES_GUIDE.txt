================================================================================
  HOW TO ADD PROJECTS, EXPERIENCES & PHOTOS IN THE FUTURE (EASY GUIDE)
================================================================================

You can easily update your website at any time without needing any AI assistance!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO CHANGE YOUR PROFILE PHOTOS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hero Section Photo (Main Circle on Home Page):
  Replace the image in: GeorgePortfolio/photos/hero_photo.jpg

- About Me Section Photo (Page 2 Photo Box):
  Replace the image in: GeorgePortfolio/photos/about_photo.jpg

- Patent Seal / Logo:
  Replace the image in: GeorgePortfolio/photos/patent_logo.png

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO UPDATE YOUR RESUME / CV:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Replace the PDF file in:
GeorgePortfolio/resume/George_Joseph_Resume.pdf

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO ADD A NEW PROJECT:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open 'ADD_NEW_PROJECT/PROJECT_TEMPLATE.txt'.
2. Copy the text block.
3. Open 'index.html' in Notepad.
4. Search for: <!-- NEW PROJECTS COMING SOON -->
5. Paste your copied project block right ABOVE that line!
6. Edit the Title, Description, and Tech Stack tags in Notepad and save!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO ADD A NEW EXPERIENCE / INTERNSHIP:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open 'ADD_EXPERIENCE/EXPERIENCE_TEMPLATE.txt'.
2. Copy the text block.
3. Open 'index.html' in Notepad, search for: <!-- ADD EXPERIENCE -->
4. Paste the block, fill in Company Name & Role, and save!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW TO ADD A NEW ACHIEVEMENT / AWARD:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open 'ADD_ACHIEVEMENT/ACHIEVEMENT_TEMPLATE.txt'.
2. Copy the text block.
3. Open 'index.html' in Notepad, search for: <!-- UPCOMING ACHIEVEMENTS -->
4. Paste the block right ABOVE that line, fill in Award Name & Year, and save!
