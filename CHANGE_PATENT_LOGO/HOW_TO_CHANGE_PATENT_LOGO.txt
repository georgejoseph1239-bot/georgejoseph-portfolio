============================================================
  HOW TO CHANGE / UPDATE THE PATENT LOGO / SEAL
============================================================

To change the Patent Logo image on your portfolio website:

METHOD 1 (Easiest - Using photos folder):
  1. Prepare your new patent logo/seal image.
  2. Name it 'patent_logo.png' (or 'patent_logo.jpg').
  3. Replace the file inside the 'photos' folder:
     GeorgePortfolio/photos/patent_logo.png
  4. Refresh your website in your browser!

METHOD 2 (Using CHANGE_PATENT_LOGO folder):
  1. Put your new image in this 'CHANGE_PATENT_LOGO' folder.
  2. Copy it into the 'photos' folder as 'patent_logo.png'.

Supported Formats: .png, .jpg, .svg, .webp
Optimal Dimensions: Square image (e.g., 500x500 px or 800x800 px) with transparent or dark background.
